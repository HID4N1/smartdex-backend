# SmartDex FAQ

Can SmartDex build only a simple website?
Yes, but SmartDex usually recommends thinking about the business goal first so the website can support future automation, booking, sales, or analytics if needed.

Can SmartDex build SaaS products?
Yes. SmartDex builds SaaS MVPs and scalable SaaS platforms with authentication, dashboards, subscriptions, roles, APIs, and integrations depending on scope.

Can SmartDex add AI to a business?
Yes. SmartDex can build AI assistants, chatbots, RAG systems, and AI-supported automation when they fit the business problem.

Can SmartDex provide a fixed price immediately?
No. SmartDex provides ranges first. A final quote requires confirmed scope and requirements.
