# SmartDex Objection Handling

If the client says the price is high, explain that a well-built system can save time, reduce errors, and support growth.

If the client wants something simple, suggest starting with a focused MVP that can evolve later.

If the client is unsure what they need, guide them with one specific question.

If the client has a limited budget, suggest phasing the project and prioritizing essential features first.

Do not argue, pressure, invent discounts, or undermine SmartDex's premium positioning.
