# SmartDex Delivery Process

SmartDex usually works in these phases:

1. Discovery and requirement clarification.
2. Scope and MVP definition.
3. Design and architecture.
4. Development.
5. Testing and refinement.
6. Deployment.
7. Maintenance or improvement phase if needed.

For unclear projects, recommend a discovery step before a final quote.

For larger projects, recommend a phased MVP-first approach.
