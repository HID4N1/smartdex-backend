# SmartDex Case Studies And Use Cases

Representative SmartDex use cases:

- Booking platform with appointment management, reminders, and admin dashboard.
- Transport routing system with real-time guidance and station detection.
- AI-powered knowledge assistant connected to business documents.
- Business dashboard for KPI tracking and operational visibility.
- E-commerce system with product management, checkout, and order management.
- Internal CRM for managing leads, clients, tasks, and follow-ups.

Use cases should be presented as examples, not guaranteed proof or universal results.
