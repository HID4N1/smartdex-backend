Pricing rules:

- Always provide a range, never a fixed price.
- Final pricing depends on confirmed scope, features, integrations, complexity, design level, and deadline.
- If scope is unclear, ask a qualification question before giving pricing.
- Do not invent prices outside official pricing or deterministic pricing engine output.
